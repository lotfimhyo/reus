# Reus Code of Conduct

**Project:** Reus
**Founder:** Lotfi Mahiddine
**Organization:** Reulink

## Our standard

The Reus community is expected to be respectful, evidence-led, and constructive.
We welcome disagreement about designs and claims when it is expressed with
technical clarity and respect for people.

## Expected behavior

Participants should communicate professionally, credit others' work, disclose
conflicts or limitations that affect a claim, and help maintain a space that is
safe for contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of private data,
deliberate misinformation, attempts to bypass safeguards, and disclosure of
secrets or unpatched vulnerabilities are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to **Contact@reulink.app**. The founder or a
delegated maintainer will review the report, protect confidentiality where
possible, and may issue a warning, remove content, limit participation, or
remove access. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

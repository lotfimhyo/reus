# Reus Project Governance

**Project:** Reus
**Founder and final maintainer:** Lotfi Mahiddine
**Organization:** Reulink

## Purpose

Reus is governed to keep ambitious AI engineering accountable to people,
evidence, and explicit operational boundaries. Governance exists to make safety
and maintainability part of delivery rather than a post-release promise.

## Decision model

The founder retains final approval for releases, licensing, public visibility,
security policy, brand use, model/provider integration, cloud-resource
creation, payment-related connectors, and remote-device-control features.
Maintainers may review and merge routine documentation, tests, and defect fixes
only within an approved scope.

## Evidence and transparency

Public capability claims require dated, reproducible evidence. Changes to trust
boundaries, data handling, privileged actions, or distributed consensus require
tests and an English documentation update. Known limitations must be described
plainly rather than presented as completed features.

## Community evolution

When the repository is public and licensed, Reulink may establish maintainer
roles and a transparent proposal process. Until then, this private repository
is a controlled preparation space, not an invitation to redistribute code or
claim project affiliation.
